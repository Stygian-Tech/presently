# Presently — Google Play listing

## App name

Presently

## Short description

Share a photo to your AT Protocol story—simply, without another feed.

## Full description

Presently is a camera-first way to share a photo to your AT Protocol story.

Open the app, take a photo, review it, and publish it as a Flashes-compatible
story record. Presently keeps the workflow focused on what you want to share
right now instead of adding another attention-driven feed.

Presently supports AT Protocol account sign-in, optional saving to your photo
library, safe retry for pending posts, and a direct feedback board for beta
testers.

Stories are public repository data. Compatible apps stop displaying them after
their 24-hour story window, but independently operated services may retain
indexed, cached, or replicated copies.

Presently is an independent AT Protocol client and is currently beta software.

## Beta release notes

Welcome to the Presently Android beta. Capture and review a photo, connect an
AT Protocol account, publish a Flashes-compatible story, choose whether to save
a local copy, and send feedback directly from Settings.
